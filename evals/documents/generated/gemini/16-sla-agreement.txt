SOFTWARE MAINTENANCE AND SLA AGREEMENT

Contract Ref: SLA-2026-MRH

Parties:
- Meridian Rail Holdings AD ("Client")
- TrackTech Systems B.V., Netherlands ("Provider")

Effective Date: 1 July 2026

1. SCOPE OF SERVICES
Provider agrees to supply Level 2 and Level 3 software maintenance for the RailFleet Manager Platform.

2. SERVICE LEVEL TARGETS
- Severity 1 (Critical Outage): Initial response within 1 hour; resolution target 4 hours.
- Severity 2 (Major Impairment): Initial response within 4 hours; resolution target 24 hours.
- Severity 3 (Minor Bug): Initial response within 24 hours; resolution in next scheduled release.

3. FEES AND BILLING
Annual maintenance fee: EUR 36,000, payable quarterly in advance (EUR 9,000 per quarter).

4. PENALTIES
Failure to meet Severity 1 resolution target on more than 2 occasions per calendar quarter results in a 5% credit on the subsequent quarterly invoice.

5. JURISDICTION
Governed by the laws of the Netherlands.
