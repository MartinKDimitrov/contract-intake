QUALITY AUDIT REPORT

Audit Reference: QMS-2026-1587
Audit Date: 19 June 2026

Audited Organization:
TransBalkan Rail EOOD
Plovdiv, Bulgaria

Audit Scope:
Rolling stock maintenance, spare parts management, and operational safety procedures.

Applicable Standard:
ISO 9001:2015 / ECM Regulation (EU) 2019/779

Summary of Results:
The audit identified two minor non-conformities and four observations.

Minor Non-Conformities:
1. Calibration certificates for three measuring devices were expired.
2. Training records for two newly hired technicians were incomplete.

Observations:
- Improved digital logging of daily inspections recommended.
- Supplier performance scoring matrix could be expanded.
- Risk assessment for temporary storage areas needs update.
- Document control procedure for work instructions requires clarification.

Corrective Action Deadline:
15 August 2026

Lead Auditor:
M. Hoffmann

Final Status:
Corrective action plan accepted.
