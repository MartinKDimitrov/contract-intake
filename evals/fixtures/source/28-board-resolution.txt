BOARD RESOLUTION

Resolution No. 2026-08/17
Date: 12 August 2026

Company:
Black Sea Logistics AD
Registered Office: Varna, Bulgaria

The Board of Directors resolves:

1. To approve the purchase of 24 freight wagons from Alpine Rail Fleet GmbH.
2. To authorize the Chief Executive Officer to execute the purchase agreement.
3. The maximum approved acquisition budget is EUR 2,160,000.
4. The transaction shall be financed from the company's available operating funds.

The resolution enters into force on the date of adoption.
