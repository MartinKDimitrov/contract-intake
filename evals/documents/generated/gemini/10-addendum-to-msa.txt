ADDENDUM NO. 1
TO MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT

This Addendum No. 1 is entered into on 1 August 2026, amending the Master Services Agreement dated 14 March 2026 between:

Meridian Rail Holdings AD ("Customer") and
Nordwind Logistik GmbH ("Supplier").

WHEREAS the parties wish to amend certain terms of the Agreement, it is hereby agreed:

1. AMENDMENT TO TERM (Section 1)
The initial term is hereby extended by an additional twelve (12) months, now ending on 13 March 2029.

2. AMENDMENT TO LIABILITY CAP (Section 3)
Section 3 of the Agreement is replaced in its entirety with:
"The aggregate liability of either party under this Agreement shall not exceed EUR 750,000."

3. RATIFICATION
All other terms and conditions of the Master Services Agreement shall remain unchanged and in full force and effect.

IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the parties have executed this Addendum.
