ADDENDUM NO. 1
TO FRAMEWORK MAINTENANCE AGREEMENT

This Addendum No. 1 is entered into on 2 August 2026, amending the Framework Maintenance Agreement dated 12 January 2026 between:

Danube Freight AD ("Customer") and RailTech Service GmbH ("Contractor").

1. AMENDMENT TO SCOPE
The Contractor shall additionally provide bogie overhaul services for up to 40 wagons per calendar year.

2. AMENDMENT TO FEES
The fixed annual fee is increased to EUR 185,000. Variable rates for bogie overhauls remain unchanged.

3. PERFORMANCE GUARANTEE
The Contractor shall provide a performance bond of EUR 50,000 valid for the remaining term of the Agreement.

4. RATIFICATION
All other terms and conditions of the original Agreement remain in full force and effect.
