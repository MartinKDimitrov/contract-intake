WAGON LEASE AGREEMENT / MIETVERTRAG

Dated / Datum: 9 June 2026

Parties / Parteien:
1. Central European Rail Assets AG, Switzerland ("Lessor" / "Vermieter")
2. Meridian Rail Transport AD, Bulgaria ("Lessee" / "Mieter")

Clause 1: Object of Lease / § 1 Mietgegenstand
En: The Lessor leases 22 Covered Freight Wagons type Hbbins.
De: Der Vermieter vermietet 22 gedeckte Güterwagen vom Typ Hbbins.

Clause 2: Monthly Rent / § 2 Monatlicher Mietzins
En: The monthly rent per wagon is EUR 1,150 (total EUR 25,300/month).
De: Der monatliche Mietzins pro Wagen beträgt EUR 1.150 (gesamt EUR 25.300/Monat).

Clause 3: Maintenance / § 3 Instandhaltung
Maintenance shall be performed by the Lessee in accordance with applicable ECM regulations and manufacturer recommendations.

Governing Law / Anwendbares Recht: Swiss Law / Schweizerisches Recht.
Term: 36 months from delivery date.
