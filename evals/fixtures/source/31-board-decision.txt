BOARD DECISION

Decision No. 2026-07/09
Date: 28 July 2026

Company:
Carpathian Logistics SA
Registered Office: Bucharest, Romania

The Board of Directors decides:

1. To approve the long-term lease of 18 tank wagons from Central European Rail Assets AG.
2. To authorize the Chief Operating Officer to sign the lease agreement and related security documents.
3. The maximum annual lease commitment shall not exceed EUR 285,000.
4. Funding shall be provided from the company’s operational cash flow and existing credit line.

This decision takes effect immediately upon adoption.
